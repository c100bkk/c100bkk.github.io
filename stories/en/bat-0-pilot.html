That was a kinda strange place. It reminded me a bit of watching the series from the last episode to the first one. Everyone here seems to have life behind them, me also. But even if you watch the serie in the wrong order you still discover the history of each episode in the correct way. Those are stories of hostel guests. We are over, but for 60 minutes a day we are able to keep our heads up.

abby i bobby

I met them twice but I still don’t know them, it’s like that with every hostel guest. For the first time I saw granny and men in the age, where someone might be 32, or something between 32+15. Abby had problems with walking, but for some reason, she was a British old lady living in a hostel located in the steep Iberian wilderness. Oh and Bobby. Between 32 and 47 years of age, spreads out waterproof cloth of working age. But somehow he instead of collapsing on that, he stepped on it. He hid below it and any shifts or jobs had no rain on him. The second time I met them was when my bladder woke me up around 2 am. I was leaving the room and already smelt god save the queen. The Royal monarchy, like it was nothing, was cooking beans, sausages and eggs. I quickly did my things and went back to my room feeling a bit ashamed that I interrupted the culture ceremony. I was a light, they were silverfishes, but this time, insects had power over human blinding eyes. Before I tell you more about British majesty and others, let me introduce… myself. I'm the princess of cleanness and this history narrator.

I arrived at the hostel three weeks ago, and that was the only place that gave me a job. Well, it gave me a roof over my head as a prison cell with two standing fans fighting against hot air. So I’m also now a princess from La mancha! That was the only job I could find, yeeeees I’m volunteering in the middle of nowhere. From 10 to 15 I clean this tiny hostel. After work I read on the roof and I’m happy that I can share my world with Abby, Bobby and others funky strangers. Sometimes I take a bus and go by the sea, but not so often, cause is much more expensive than roof-reading, and I don’t earn money. But, am no complaining! Yes, actually I feel like I am taking care of my professional career! So I guess maybe in the future this internship will help me. Cause well, I am a cleaning lady, or maybe more truly I want to be a professional cleaning lady. My career adventure with cleaning starts relatively late, only after my master's graduation. So I need to make up for lost years. I studied, because my family my whole life tells me: study, study, study in one and only thing you need to care about now. So I listened to them and most of my youth I had been learning. But in the end, all I had from that was being pretty safe with finals exams results. To spice it up, I am not so smart! Of course if you learn a lot, you will know something. But till today, this phrase really pissed me off: “smart but lazy”. That’s not me. I was hard-working and stupid. People like me can only have good results from things you can learn by heart. So after high school I couldn’t go to technical uni or to the other academic place for producing craved employees. But with help from my older sister I found a loophole in the system – study of bla bla bla communication. A real phenomenon and hen’s teeth in the East Europe university landscape. It was truly amazing. At 9:15 a.m. they teach us about how bad capitalism is, it’s like a bad egg, which is poisoning the lives of normal people. A lot of difficult words, shocking terms and ideas taken from French sociologists and philosophers of France. But the same day at 12:00 they showed us techniques, how to sell that egg and tell people that actually it’s good and tasty. It confused me a bit. My silly-black-and-white-mind was lost. So even though I got excellent grades and a scholarship for a presentation about launching a new influencer self-brand… I finished studying with knowledge and skills, which I didn’t want to use. But it’s not like that, I am a revolutionary, who stands on a barricade built with cups full of batch coffee from Rwanda. If someone gave me 3k for a ketchup brand ad on Valentine's Day, I would write a song “tomato, tomato, lady”. However, that never happened. I was a terrible intern. My bosses, creative directors, senior copywriters didn’t see potential in my copy about protein dessert for social media. Slogan: “Don’t wait for the water in the desert, run for protein dessert”. They told me that it could be claimed by every grocery brand and they don’t see its unique value like: “honesty, positive-energy and equality”. Well, probably they were right. I’m easily giving up, and critical feedback about the value of yoghurt was too much for me. So I used it to light my flash in the pan for the creative industry. From that fire I smoked my thin LM cig, wiped off blurred mascara and went back to home, to my hometown. That was the end of my great dona draper career…tbc
