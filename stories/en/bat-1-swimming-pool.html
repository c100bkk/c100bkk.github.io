฿ 1 — Swimming Pool

I thought it would be an awful time, but always when I expect the worst scenarios, life somehow turns my negative manifestation into something good. I found my passion, work and I started getting horny. I had been a cleaning lady for several months at the swimming pool, I liked it a lot. Cig before work with Miss Steffy, running with mop and dancing with tears in my eyes in my ears, chlorine inhalation. The last one occurred my biggest, and till now, only sexual affection. However, honeymoons can't last forever. Mum also had tears in eyes, cause her daughter after college had been cleaning the swimming pool. Her sadness had been spoiling my happiness. I thought, well, I am a handsome, ambitious girl after uni and Miss Steffa ended my unspoken words with: You can’t spend the rest of your life just cleaning an enormous shit hole. Yup, my mummy had been talking to me the same, but she had not used so lovely anti-euphemisms. That happened during cold November night. After my first sip, and Steffy's third sip of beer. We were sitting on the stairs by our swimming pool. That was our birthday party. Mine is on the first day of November, and Steff’s is on the last day of the month. I was 25, she was 60, so on the 15th of November we celebrated our 42,5 years old. I just finished my five years academic arc, she was five years before retirement. I like noticing little things like that. People don’t care about that, but I think life is kinda more heart-warming, when you appreciate charming coincidences. For example, your best friend from primary school had the same distance to there, as you. But she needs to go to the left and you to the right. And you have commitment to walk everyday to the gate, which is a few metres away, just for the accuracy of your calculations. It doesn’t make your friendship more special? We need to care about thighs like that! Yup, but let’s go back to the topic, well, what I was talking about? Ah, yes, we were sitting there in a bit of a pathetic mood. Somebody could say, just a normal Friday after a late shift, but for us a unique bday! Just Friday on the cold stairs of a crappy sport object, but it had been renovated thanks to EU funding earmarked for the development of urban-rural municipalities. Pay me for ad motherfuckers. Someone wrote on the internet one day: I suppose that I’ll never be happier, than when I was drinking raspberry vodka at the frozen garden with friends, in my 17 years old. I know maybe it’s naive romanisation and it’s not so cool actually. But, we polish people build our modern culture on post soviet esthetic. So let me enjoy the smell of kebab and excitement of boys before drinking night. But it only works in towns without trams. Sorry, but in bigger cities it is not the same. Of course you have your own sentimental flowers, but being limited to just a few streets teaches small-town boys to use their imagination. Again, let’s back to Steffy words. She is an amazing woman, just like everybody, a bit. She doesn’t treat her job like a passion as I do. She would rather sit at the office, but well it is, what it is. She would probably turn off you boring Bergman and have fun with disco-polo shows. But all of that in the end doesn't count. She was just a cool, honest, funny and SYMPHATETIC person. Trivial? and what’s, our talks were mostly trivials, but somehow she was the one, who could make me realise that I need a change. Those perfect days have expiry dates and soon the small-town imperative of normality will make me sick. She threw a widow's mite to the fountain of my enormous shit hole. So I left, but I didn’t give up cleaning. I still wanted to prove to the world that it’s a sophisticated piece of work. I knew a bit of Spanish and I went, where dancing and cleaning seemed to me more sheltered by the eternal sun. It’s not easy. Unemployment is high here, and as you can see, it’s hard for a princess with a master’s degree, but having little experience in the sector, to find a job. But this time I can’t give up. I had my silly pride, (and years of therapy behind me, and antidepressants in my handbag). I’m meeting new people, and fuck me, I will be selling thier histoires.

There must be a life after being 17 years old and frozen fruity vodka.
