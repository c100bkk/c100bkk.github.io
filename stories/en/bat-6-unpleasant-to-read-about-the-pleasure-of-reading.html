฿ 6 — Unpleasant to Read, About the Pleasure of Reading

October 24, 2026

Being a spy demands patience, huge things dance with the monotony of powerless days. So, today I will tell you what I am doing after my two jobs. READING! Until quite recently I haven’t been a bookworm at all. But one day after I made a cold calculation and read some, not so smart, articles on “how to live”, I knew that walking in the fresh air is life changing shit and reading is the sun of hobbies. Well, both of these revealed truths are partly bullshit, nevertheless I was trying to practice them. I was especially fascinated by books, but in my personal way. Thier seductive ostensible egalitarianism and possibility of reading every time and everywhere made me a future fake-it-till-you-make-it-reader. A breakthrough moment happened during a cloudy grey afternoon at the bus station. I asked my study friend, who was, is, and will be forever my alpha and omega, what’s her fav book. She answered Hopscotch. I borrowed, read, and loved that amazing novel. After that I was able to choose books on my own. I just was looking at something written by a oldschool latin americo guy, and in most cases my pick occurred be a banger. I was also forced to do that, because the Hopscotch made me a bit sick… and my alpha and omega, for the sake of my health, refused to recommend more books. All these situations and some other life-clouds made, that now I am reading In Evil Hour on the hostel roof, which also has the functionality of drying space and ascetic lounge area. In the Polish version of this text, here I am writing a huge and foolish digression of my long sentences, which made texts unpleasant to read. However, because I don’t know at all how interpuncation works in english, I’ll spare you this part, oh but I only need to regard my friend, which last time suggested me that maybe I should sometimes use less commas and use more other punctuation marks – Forgive me, my dear, I try to fight with this bad behaviour, but it’s a reason why I stole nickname “from La Mancha”. Unfortunately, without that whole part, I can’t smoothly connect the rest of the story. Forgive me, my English readers.

On this roof I am reading a piece written by Gabriel García Márquez. Am I flexing? Guess so! I can’t tell you more about the book, than just mention who is the author. Describing novel without making spoilers is so draining, almost like this fucking chess play with the sun here. I am forced to change a table looking shaded H2 every half hour. You know, my main hero will be other than yours. My man, beside of what Márquez put on him, is affected by my own currently obsession on british conspiration, afro instrumental, which I am listening during reading, this roof-patio with vibes of some sandy netflix drug series and the cacophony of the many voices of the guests smoking cigarettes here, AND probably many more. So I dare you, your hero, if you are for example a baker reading a book with mf doom instrumental in some Stockholm modern art gallery while waiting for your first date with an artsy chick will be different. I have no right to spoil your play with Márquez imagine. Nevertheless I can tell you what my read-game brought me. I finished the book when it was already dark around, and finally my princess moon checked the sun. I have to go to sleep. But the lovely cool air and matryoshka dolls of negation, which the book gave me, stimulated me. I’m smoking a cigar and opening my eyes for darkness. Sounds fucking cheap, but yheaa sometimes is like that. Moonkillsun, is it possible mister Kozelek? Foxes are tracked at night…tbc…
