฿ 3 — Bakery

September 24, 2026

Who gets up early, can drink a coffee before work. Who is lucky, has a lil coffee bakery 300m from home. And who is super lucky, has there coffee for a song and delicious sweet pastries. I don’t have a home, but a coffee place like that is within spitting distance from my hostel, so I feel like I am lucky enough. I go there every morning, or more honestly something about 9:30. I say espresso por favor and I point at some sweet treat. Yesterday I took a crescent roll with chocolate, but palm oil from nutella sank my cerebral ganglia and tons of sugar heaped up jejune island. So today I chose danish pastry with a slice of an apple. It’s so enjoyable to cut it with a special knife, that is too sharp for butter and too stupid for bread. But the real sweetness stands by the cash. I don’t know her name, so let’s say she’s Aphrodite of kindness. She's an older woman, but not in a granny way, however she might sometimes play with her grandchild, if only her own child had not contributed to the demographic crisis, and of course if only she had done that before by herself. I feel really safe and comfy here, drinking coffee, dangling one's leg and collecting smiles from Aphrodite. This smile is special because the bakery is almost empty and she’s not over stimulated by customers. I don’t mean that during peak hours she starts to be rude, no, no, she is able to handle it… with a smile also. But the lazy one smile is something else. I could compare it to the difference between a brain damaging choco-sugar bomb from yesterday and a more sustainable snack for brain chemistry from today. That sounds a bit like me suggesting that everyday passing by smiles are a bit harmful. But honestly, they are. You’re sad and lonely and start thinking about every flighty lips, and you can be even more depressed that after all nothing is left of them. And a more calm, personal, longer than 3,5 second smile stays with you, just like that one boring, but cheerful piece of art, created by some great painter. When I was musing that way I noticed that we’re no longer alone with Aphrodite. Abby was sitting on her wheelchair at the other end of the coffee room. Not a sign of Bobby, how she managed to arrive here and climbed the steep streets, I don’t have any idea. I paid much attention to watch that genre scene, but nothing really changed. Just like someone cut her from the hostel lobby and pasted here. She was reading something with a stooped closed figure, milk camouflaged her drink. Unbitten pastry hid filling, and Abby hid feelings and remained the great mysterious. I didn’t have more time for an investigation. My late morning was in need to transform to the punctual start of the cleaning. My career first of all!
